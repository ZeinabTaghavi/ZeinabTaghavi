## Hi, I am Zeinab Sadat Taghavi

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer and Communication Sciences (EDIC) at [EPFL](https://www.epfl.ch/en/), working in the [LiGHT lab](https://www.light-laboratory.org/) under the supervision of Dr. [Mary-Anne Hartley](https://people.epfl.ch/mary-anne.hartley). I am also an associate researcher at [HSLU](https://www.hslu.ch/en/), where I work with Dr. [Andreas Marfurt](https://www.hslu.ch/en/lucerne-university-of-applied-sciences-and-arts/about-us/people-finder/profile/?pid=5550), who co-supervises my research.

My research focuses on reasoning and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) with large language models, with a broader interest in long-context reasoning and trustworthy machine learning.

Before EPFL, I was a research assistant at [LMU Munich](https://www.lmu.de/en/) with Prof. [Hinrich Schütze](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=qIL9dWUAAAAJ&hl=en). I hold an M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence from [Sharif University of Technology](https://en.sharif.ir/).
